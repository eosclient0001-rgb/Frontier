Method

  • One fixed Sobol' (0,2) point set shared by every pixel — no RNG, no per-pixel noise.
  • Every lobe traced: coat, specular and diffuse; glass splits into reflection and refraction.
  • No Russian roulette: fixed depth limits and a fixed throughput cutoff.
  • Softboxes integrated by light quadrature + BSDF quadrature, power-heuristic MIS.
  • Error is structured and shrinks every power of two; it never sparkles.
