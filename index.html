Frontier · combined SDF terrain engine

This build fuses two earlier experiments: GPU particle erosion on a true 3D signed-distance volume (realistic carving), and field-pass erosion (stream-power incision, sediment routing, thermal talus) for large-scale drainage structure.

  • Anti-pit guards — capacity-limited detachment, shear thresholds, hardness armoring, a resting-agent deposit rule, and a per-cell regenerating cut budget stop agents from drilling bottomless holes.
  • Anti-blur passes — diffusion is gated off inside channels (flow-area mask), incision uses a sharp n≈1.5 law, and a detail-sharpen pass re-injects ridge micro-relief on fresh rock.
  • Proper SDF sculpting — density and CSG brushes edit the volume directly, then a banded Eikonal solver re-normalizes distances.
