01Paint where the rock should fracture

Drag across visible rock. Paint follows your stroke; depth extends into the rock along your view. Smaller chunks produce a denser fracture network. Radius affects new paint samples.

Intact cracks only mark piece boundaries. The volume stays unchanged until you remove a selection. One active map; a new map replaces it.

Open-gap mode removes rock along every crack (minimum 0.8 m on this grid) and can detach chunks. Use Intact cracks to leave the formation joined.

Renderer diagnostics

        

          The scene uses WebGL2. Particle motion, erosion, deposition, and
          sculpting run on the GPU using WebGL2 fragment passes. A worker
          handles initial generation and explicit connected-fragment selection.
          WebGPU is not initialized. A floating-point render target is required
          for GPU erosion.
