FRONTIER GP

WIDE-TRACK ARCADE RACING · MODERN CITY · INFINITE

An endless, procedurally-built anti-grav-style circuit through a modern daylight city — wide banked track, forked race lines, bridges and a living railway network running beside, above and straight through your race. Your car drives with free Need-for-Speed-style arcade physics: real steering, throttle, braking and handbrake drifts. No lanes. No on-rails runner controls.

✓19 m-wide WipEout-style track — banked curves, crests, red/white kerbs
✓Y-splits — fork left / right, then merge back
✓Up/down splits — low line vs. elevated bridge line
✓Parallel ground railway with oncoming trains
✓Parallel elevated monorail in the air, same-direction trains
✓Perpendicular ground crossings — trains cut through the track!
✓Perpendicular aerial rail bridges — trains pass overhead
✓Overhead rail/sign gantries, bridges on pillars, modern city
✓Infinite seeded generation — chunks stream & recycle forever
