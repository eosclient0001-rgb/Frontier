∇ FIELDWORK FIELD GUIDE · 01

A five-pass formula read.

When an equation in a paper feels impenetrable, slow it down. You don't need to remember every identity; you need a reliable way into the notation.

  1. 01
    Name the quantities.

    Scalar, vector, field, or rate? Write down the units beside each symbol.

  2. 02
    Translate the operators.

    A derivative measures change; ∇ points uphill; ∇ · measures net outflow; ∇ × measures local spin.

  3. 03
    Read left to right.

    The left often says what is changing. The right often says why, or what balances it.

  4. 04
    Check the units.

    Every term being added must have matching dimensions. Units catch missing factors fast.

  5. 05
    Try an easy case.

    Set the flow to zero, make a field uniform, or remove a force. Does the equation behave as expected?

A familiar example · incompressible flow
∂u/∂t + (u · ∇)u = −∇p/ρ + ν∇²u + f

Say it in one breath: “The fluid's acceleration equals pressure, viscosity, and applied forces.” Then inspect each term. That's the whole course in miniature.
