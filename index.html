Anatomical Structure

Scientific Anatomy & Biomechanical Reference Dossier

This 3D model replicates the morphology and biomechanics of the Order Scorpiones (specifically Emperor Scorpion Pandinus imperator and Asian Forest Scorpion Heterometrus spinifer) with exact anatomical segmentation, 66 articulated skeletal nodes, PBR chitin shaders, and high-speed strike kinematics.

1. Prosoma (Cephalothorax)

Shield carapace with anterior median notch. Bears 2 median ocelli on an elevated tubercle and 3 pairs of lateral ocelli. Underneath lies the pentagonal ventral sternum, and chelicerae (anterior grasping jaws) for macerating prey.

2. Raptorial Pedipalps & Chelae

Composed of Coxa, Trochanter, Femur (humerus) with granulated carinae, Patella (brachium / elbow), and massive Chela. The Chela consists of a swollen manus (palm), fixed finger with serrated denticles, and articulated movable finger (dactyl) capable of crushing prey.

3. Mesosoma (7 Articulated Tergites)

Somites II to VIII form 7 distinct dorsal plates (Tergites 1–7) overlapping sequentially. Ventral sternites on segments 3–7 feature oblique slits (stigmata) for book lung respiration. Segment 2 bears paired sensory pectines with individual comb teeth.

4. Metasoma (5 Tail Rings) & Telson

Five cylindrical caudal segments (Cauda I–V), each strengthened by 8 longitudinal keels (carinae) with granular pustules. Segment V features pronounced ventral keels. Terminates in the telson vesicle (paired venom glands) and hypodermic aculeus stinger.

5. Alternating Tetrapod Gait Biomechanics

Scientific literature (Bowerman 1975, 1981; Spagna 2011) documents an alternating tetrapod gait: Tetrapod A (R1, L2, R3, L4) alternates in anti-phase with Tetrapod B (L1, R2, L3, R4), with a ~10% rear-to-front metachronal wave and tail counterbalance sway.

6. High-Speed Strike Kinematics

High-speed video analysis (Coelho et al. 2017, Functional Ecology) revealed defensive stinger strikes reach velocities over 100–128 cm/s in open whip trajectories. The attack animation models the full predatory sequence: wind-up rearing, pincer clamp, overhead stinger plunge, venom pulse, and snap retraction.
