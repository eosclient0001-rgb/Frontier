HOW THIS OCEAN WORKS

A game-ready, fully procedural open ocean. No textures, no tiling — every pixel of water, foam and sky is simulated or synthesised on the GPU each frame. v2 uses a radial camera-following mesh (no projected-grid stretch artifacts) with a true horizon and fog skirt.

WAVES — GPU FFT, 2 CASCADES

  • Tessendorf (2001) spectrum evaluated per-frame, transformed by a radix-2 butterfly IFFT in fragment shaders (2 × 256², ping-pong float targets).
  • Phillips wind sea + narrow swell lobe, deep-water dispersion ω = √(g·k), wavenumber-weighted choppy displacement.
  • Cascades live on incommensurate tiles (480 m + 97.3 m, rotated 37°) with a smooth wavenumber crossfade — the multiscale anti-tiling approach of Gugi 2025 / Poseidon 2025.

FOAM, SPRAY & SPLASH

  • Whitecaps from the Jacobian determinant of the horizontal displacement (crest pinch), persisted in a rate-limited, capped foam buffer that cannot saturate the sea.
  • Breakup uses domain-rotated procedural noise streaked along the wind — infinite and tile-free.
  • 4,096 spray + 1,536 splash particles, stateless on GPU, emitted only on breaking crests; splash jets are ballistic and velocity-stretched in screenspace.
  • A moored buoy and cruising patrol boat ride the same simulated waves (CPU samples the live FFT field for true buoyancy) and the boat leaves a foam wake; optional wind-blown rain.

SHADING

  • Exact dielectric Fresnel (n = 1.34) with distance roughness, sun glitter, crest subsurface scattering, procedural sky with animated clouds reflected in the water, height-aware fog.
  • 4× MSAA HDR pipeline, ACES tone mapping, vignette + film grain.
