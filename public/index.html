Frontier Road Editor

ProjectZero-style experimental editor for building NFS-scale road networks — roads only, clean topology, full street detail.

Navigation (Unreal-style)

  • Right-drag look · W A S D fly · Q / E down / up
  • Shift boost · mouse wheel base speed · F focus selection
  • T top-down toggle

Editing

  • 2 Roads tool — pick a preset in the Road library, click to lay spline points, double-click / Enter to finish, Esc cancels. Clicking an existing node snaps to it.
  • 3 Rails tool — same, but lays a railway (sleepers, rails, automatic level crossings).
  • 4 Props tool — pick furniture, click to place (bollards, bumps, lamps, signals, signs, portals…).
  • 5 Erase — click roads, rails or props to delete.
  • 1 Select — click a road to edit profile / bridge / details, click a node disc to edit elevation.
  • Delete removes the selection · Ctrl+Z undo.

City generator

Seeded procedural city: street grid, arterials with bus + cycle + parking lanes, diagonal boulevard, ring road, highway, railway with level crossings, metro portals, lamps, signals, bollards, speed bumps and zebra crossings.

Export

Export OBJ / Export GLB bake the whole map (roads, curbs, markings, props) into one file you can drop into Unreal / Blender for your NFS-style map.
